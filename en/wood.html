WOOD DIVISION

It all began with a single tree.

Since our founding in 1950, we have worked hand in hand with wood. From log procurement to sawmilling and assembly — all in-house.

SCROLL

From manufacturing and selling wooden pallets, crates, and packaging materials to contract packing.

FOREST’s Lumber Division has worked with wood in Yamaga, Kumamoto for 75 years since our founding. We choose the right material for each application, from domestic Japanese cedar to New Zealand pine and LVL, and build custom, made-to-order products to fit each customer’s cargo. Handling everything in-house, from log procurement through sawmilling and assembly, is what makes our short lead times and flexible proposals possible.

Our Products

01

Wooden Pallet Manufacturing

We manufacture a wide range of wooden pallets from domestic Japanese cedar, New Zealand pine, LVL, and other materials. We can also produce hybrid pallets for specific applications and provide fumigation for export.

  • Japanese cedar
  • New Zealand pine
  • LVL
  • Fumigation
02

Wooden Crate Assembly

We design and build wooden crates to match each customer’s cargo and specifications. Our crates reduce the risk of damage in transit and keep valuable cargo well protected. We also handle one-off items such as precision equipment and large machinery.

  • Custom design
  • Export packing
  • Large cargo
03

Packaging Materials & Contract Packing

We manufacture a variety of packaging materials, mainly square timber sawn at our own mill. We cut them to the dimensions each application requires and also supply packing companies. We can take on the packing work itself on site as well.

  • Packaging materials
  • Contract packing
  • Supply to packers
