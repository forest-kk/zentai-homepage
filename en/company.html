GREETING

GREETING

Treasuring every encounter
and every connection.

In 2025, FOREST celebrated the 75th anniversary of its founding. We owe this milestone to the people of our community and to everyone we work with, and we are deeply grateful.

Looking ahead to our 80th and 90th anniversaries, and to the major milestone of our 100th, all of our employees are working together as one team to move on to the next stage.

Over our long history, we have steadily diversified, and today the company is made up of eight divisions. As the times change, we will continue to adapt flexibly to changes in society and keep taking on new challenges.

Through our work, we will treasure every encounter and every connection, and strive to contribute to the happiness of the people around us and to society. We sincerely ask for your continued support.

President & CEOHideki Oda
