HOTEL

Super Hotel Kumamoto Yamaga

The Hotel Division operates Super Hotel Kumamoto Yamaga as a franchisee of Super Hotel. The hotel opened on July 1, 2017.

The hotel has 101 guest rooms and a large natural hot spring bath, “Yamaga Toro no Yu,” and welcomes everyone who visits Yamaga.

For reservations and rates, please visit the official Super Hotel website.
