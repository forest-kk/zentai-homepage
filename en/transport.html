TRANSPORT DIVISION

From making to delivering.

The Transport Division is run by Kariku Co., Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of FOREST. In addition to deliveries for FOREST's own businesses, it handles delivery requests from customers, carrying goods across the Kyushu and Chugoku regions.

ONE-STOP

From manufacturing to transport,
all in one place.

Within FOREST's integrated logistics services, the Transport Division handles the “moving” part. Linked with manufacturing at the Lumber Division and with materials sales, storage, and packing at the Logistics & Packaging Division, it lets you consult a single point of contact for everything from sourcing materials to transport.

  1. 01

    Manufacturing

    Manufacturing of wooden pallets, packing materials, wooden crates, and more (Lumber Division)

  2. 02

    Logistics Supplies

    Plastic pallets, PP bands, cardboard, and other materials (Logistics & Packaging Division)

  3. 03

    Warehousing

    Owning and operating commercial warehouses

  4. 04

    Packing & Handling

    One-stop service from packing to inventory management

  5. 05

    Transport

    Deliveries across the Kyushu and Chugoku regions by our subsidiary Kariku

For a list of our commercial warehouses, including the Kariku warehouses (3 buildings, Yamaga City, Kumamoto Prefecture), see the Logistics & Packaging Division page.
