SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT

Protecting people, using wood to the fullest.

Safety comes before everything. We continue to build a workplace where employees can work with peace of mind, and to make sure nothing goes to waste, down to the bark and offcuts produced in sawmilling.

Five Safety Principles Rules & Training Heatstroke Prevention Using Every Part of the Wood

SAFETY FIRST

Five Safety Principles

These five principles form the foundation of safety at FOREST.

  1. 1Safety comes before everything
  2. 2Never do dangerous work, and never let others do it
  3. 3Make hazard prediction a habit
  4. 4Fix hazardous spots
  5. 5Follow the rules

RULES & EDUCATION

A workplace where the rules can always be checked.

Safety Rulebook

Total0pages

We have created a rulebook to raise safety awareness. It is divided in detail into company-wide items, items for each section, workflows, and more, and a copy is kept in every department so that it can be checked at any time.

We explain and review its contents during new-employee training and whenever the rulebook is revised.

  • Training for managers and supervisors by outside instructors

HEATSTROKE PREVENTION

Heatstroke Prevention

Recent extreme summer heat has raised the risk of heatstroke at work. FOREST strengthens its preventive measures every year to keep the workplace safe.

  • 0units

    Spot coolers

  • 0units

    WBGT metersTo check the heat index at each workplace

  • 0employees

    Fan-cooled work clothing paid for by the companyFor those who requested it

  • 0sites

    Frozen drink machines installedLeased since 2026

Adding new measures every year

  1. 2020
    • Use of spot coolers
  2. 2021
    • Company pays for fan-cooled work clothing
  3. 2022
    • WBGT meters deployed
    • Roof improvement work
    • Response flow for heatstroke cases created
  4. 2023
    • Oral rehydration solution stocked
    • Extra breaks during the hot season
  5. 2024
    • Heatstroke prevention tablets distributed
    • Wall insulation work
  6. 2025
    • Jet fans introduced
  7. 2026
    • Frozen drink machines leased
    • Neck coolers
    • Warning signs posted

Everyone knows what to do if it happens

  1. 1If you see someone who may have heatstroke, always report it to your supervisor
  2. 2Stop work and cool the body down
  3. 3Check their level of consciousness and whether they can drink fluids on their ownIf anything seems wrong, switch to emergency response immediately
  4. 4Keep monitoring their conditionNever leave them alone during transport or while monitoring

ENVIRONMENT

Using wood to the very last.

Sawmilling produces bark, sawdust, and boards that cannot be used. Instead of throwing them away, our Lumber Division passes them on to their next use.

  1. 01

    Bark

    Made into bark compost

  2. 02

    Sawdust

    Made into fertilizer

    Used as bedding for cattle

  3. 03

    Unusable boards

    Used as raw material for paper

  4. 04

    Broken pallets

Our sawmilling process is introduced on the Lumber Division page. Since 2012, we have also been selling electricity generated by solar power.
