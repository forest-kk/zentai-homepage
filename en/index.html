About Us

From a single pallet
to an entire hotel.

FOREST was founded in 1950 in Yamaga, Kumamoto. Over the 75 years since, we have grown from a lumber business into eight divisions, adding new branches as the times changed, and today we support local life and industry across the region. Our goal is to be a leading company in our region. Valuing every encounter as a once-in-a-lifetime connection, we will continue to work for the well-being of the people we serve and to contribute to society.

HISTORY

HISTORY

75 Years, and Beyond

1950

Founded

Founded as a lumber business in Kahoku, Yamaga, Kumamoto.

1972

Incorporated

Established as a joint-stock company, strengthening the foundation of the business.

1986

Weaving business launched

Began producing woven fabrics, expanding into manufacturing.

2001

50th anniversary

Renamed FOREST Co., Ltd., entering a new half-century.

2011

Commercial warehouse license

Launched full-scale logistics operations and began building our warehouse network.

2012

Solar power sales begin

Entered the clean energy business.

2017

Hotel business launched

Opened Super Hotel Kumamoto Yamaga.

2025

75th anniversary

Three quarter-centuries behind us, we look ahead to our next milestone: our 100th anniversary.

2026

FLC Yamaga completed

FOREST Logistics Center Yamaga is completed, our hub for the future of logistics.
